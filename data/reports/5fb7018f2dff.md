# How to drive transformations with rigor

- 来源：麦肯锡
- 分类：战略/综合
- 发布：2026-09-30T00:00:00+00:00
- 原文：https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/how-to-drive-transformations-with-rigor

## 摘要

McKinsey research finds that companies executing transformations with rigor are much more likely to meet overall organizational transformation goals. Chris Hagedorn, Preeya Mody, and Michel Morenville join us to discuss what rigor really means in the context of transformation and how to embed it throughout the process, from setting the aspiration, to mobilization, to delivering impact.
